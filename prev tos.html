  • Farming is the act of intentionally getting around the PrevF1dex spawning algorithm to ensure collectibles spawn more often. There are many ways that this can be done, all are forbidden. (Making servers specifically designated for the spawning of collectibles or hiding spawn channels from all other members to ensure only a small select group can obtain them falls under this rule, so does adding the app to any servers with a member count below 10. Forcing a spawn by sending a message does not.)

5. User conduct

You agree to use PrevF1dex in a fair and lawful manner. The following behavior is strictly prohibited:

  • No cheating: Do not use any third-party programs, scripts, exploits or automation tools to gain an unfair advantage in the game.
  • No impersonation: Do not impersonate other users, moderators, application owners, or any entity.
  • No account sharing: Account sharing is prohibited and all accounts participating may have their access revoked.

Farming

The use of farming techniques is strictly prohibited and will be punished. This includes:

  • Creating servers and adding bots or inactive accounts solely to catch PrevF1dex collectibles.
  • Hiding the spawn channel in a server thus making it accessible to fewer than 10 people.
  • Using alt accounts to funnel daily rewards and achievements back to yourself.
  • Using other people’s accounts, or having other people who do not play PrevF1dex funnel daily rewards and achievements to your account.
  • Using exploits to your advantage and not reporting them. (Reporting an exploit will always get you a reward; not reporting will always get you blacklisted.)
  • Any other means of gaining an unfair advantage over legitimate players.

The application owners and PrevF1dex moderation team have full discretion to determine what constitutes cheating, farming or abusive behavior.

7. Privacy and data

PrevF1dex collects minimal user data necessary for its functionality. This typically includes:

  • Discord user IDs, used to identify you inside our database.
  • Server (guild) IDs, used to store settings necessary to the App’s operation.
  • Channel IDs, used to know in which channel collectibles should spawn.
  • A list of collectibles owned by a user of the service.
  • A history of trades done on collectibles, including the users that once owned a collectible but do not anymore.

All data is stored on a PostgreSQL server running on a Virtual Private Machine hosted by Oracle Corporation. Interaction between the App and the database server is exclusively local using Tortoise ORM.

Access to the data is restricted to the application owners. The PrevF1dex moderation team does not have direct access to the data.

You should not share personal information, including passwords, addresses or financial details, through the App.

A more detailed privacy policy that describes what data is collected and how it is handled is available via the link provided in the PrevF1dex Discord server.

You may request a copy or the deletion of your personal data held by the application by contacting the application owners in the Discord server.
